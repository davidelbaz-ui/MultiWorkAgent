MultiWorkAgent includes an AI agent that answers questions using your messages, attachments, knowledge files, and workspace context. Please read this page before you rely on anything the agent says or before you act on its suggestions. By using the agent, you acknowledge the points below. Our Terms & Conditions and Licence agreement govern liability and your legal relationship with us; this page explains what you need to know in plain language.

The agent can be wrong

Replies are produced by third-party AI models. They can be incorrect, incomplete, outdated, biased, or misleading, even when they sound confident. The agent does not “know” your business the way you do. It may invent details (“hallucinate”), misread files, or misunderstand integrations and data you mention.

Do not treat agent output as fact without checking sources, your own records, and (when appropriate) qualified professionals.

You are responsible for decisions and actions

  • Review every response before you use it for operations, finance, HR, legal, safety, or customer-facing work.
  • You choose what to do in your company, in connected tools, and in third-party systems. We are not responsible for business decisions you make based on the agent.
  • Verify numbers, dates, names, compliance steps, and technical instructions before executing them in production systems.
  • If you share agent output with teammates or customers, you are responsible for that communication.

Not professional advice

The agent is a general productivity assistant. It is not a lawyer, accountant, tax adviser, licensed financial adviser, or other regulated professional. Nothing in chat is legal, tax, investment, medical, or safety advice. Get advice from qualified professionals for those matters.

How the agent works with integrations

MultiWorkAgent is built so your AI agent can do operational work, not only answer questions. You connect third-party services and databases per business on Data & integrations. When you ask the agent to perform tasks, it can use those connections — for example calling provider APIs, reading or updating records, and running queries — as part of an execution run, within what you have connected for the active business scope and what the Service supports.

  • Connect only systems you are authorized to use; the agent can only act through connections you set up.
  • Mistakes in connected systems are possible — wrong updates, deletions, or messages — even when the agent sounds confident. Review instructions and check outcomes in the affected tools.
  • Usage limits, concurrency, and run details apply; see the run inspector and usage chip in the app.

Your data and third-party AI

Messages and attachments you send may be processed by AI providers we use to operate the Service, as described in our Privacy Policy. Do not send secrets you are not allowed to share, personal data you cannot lawfully process, or content that violates law or third-party terms.
