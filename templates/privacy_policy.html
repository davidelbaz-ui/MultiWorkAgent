Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ legal_last_updated }}

MultiWorkAgent (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the MultiWorkAgent web application and related services (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use the Service.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, authentication identifiers (including from Google or Microsoft sign-in), and team role.
  • Business workspace data — business names, settings, agent preferences, chat messages, execution runs, and usage metering records tied to your account.
  • Integration and connection data — OAuth tokens and API credentials you authorize per business, stored encrypted; we do not share credentials across businesses or accounts.
  • Database connection metadata — connection settings and schema information you configure for read or read/write access.
  • Billing information — subscription status and payment-related events processed through our payment providers (for example Square); we do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, logs, and cookies or similar technologies used for sessions and security.

How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service, including AI agent runs and integrations you request.
  • Authenticate users, enforce roles (owner, operator, viewer), and protect against abuse.
  • Meter usage, apply plan limits, and process billing where applicable.
  • Send in-app notifications and service-related communications you configure.
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests.

Third-party services

When you connect integrations (for example GitHub, Google Workspace, HubSpot, QuickBooks, or Stripe), we exchange data with those providers only as needed to perform actions you authorize. Their use of your data is governed by their privacy policies. We use infrastructure and AI providers to host the Service and run agent requests; those providers process data according to their terms and our configuration.

Data retention and security

We retain information while your account is active and as needed for legal, billing, or security purposes. You may delete workspace data or your account from Settings (owner-only for account deletion). We use encryption for integration secrets, access controls, and industry-standard measures appropriate to a hosted SaaS product.

Your choices

  • Update account and notification preferences in Settings.
  • Disconnect integrations or database connections per business.
  • Request account deletion, which removes associated data subject to backup and legal retention limits.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

International users

If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other locations where we or our providers operate.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data may be sent to privacy@multiworkagent.com.
